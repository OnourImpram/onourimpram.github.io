Reading the evidence

Selected research on spirituality and mental health. Read findings alongside study design and limits of inference.

What are we measuring?

Spirituality, religiosity, meaning and wellbeing are different constructs. When a spirituality measure incorporates wellbeing, its association with mental health may partly reflect conceptual overlap. Examine definitions and item content before interpreting a coefficient.

Association, change and treatment effects

Cross-sectional associations do not establish temporal order. Cohorts improve temporal information but cannot remove all confounding. In randomized trials, comparator choice and attrition matter. A nonsignificant overall difference in the religious CBT trial does not establish equivalence.
