Assessment directory

Check purpose, population, adaptation and permission before selecting a tool. No scales are administered or scored here.

Evaluating measurement instruments

Consider construct, sample and conditions of use together. A scale score is not a diagnosis. Publication in one language does not validate other language versions.

Brief RCOPE

14 items distinguish positive and negative religious coping.

Religious and Spiritual Struggles Scale (RSS-14)

The Turkish RSS-14 adaptation reported six dimensions in 495 Muslim participants. Total alpha was 0.82, subscales 0.60–0.82. These results do not establish invariance across worldviews or clinical cut-offs.

Meaning in Life Questionnaire (MLQ)

10 items measure presence of meaning and search for meaning separately.

DUREL

Five items address three dimensions of religiosity.

Items are not reproduced here. Before research use, check the original form, adaptation study, scoring guidance and rights holder’s terms. MARSAM grants no licence.
