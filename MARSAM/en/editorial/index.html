Editorial and review policy

How content is proposed, sourced, reviewed, translated and updated.

Editorial draft

AI-assisted text and translation. Final scientific and language review is pending.

Prepare a contribution

Claim and source in the same record

Each dossier shows sources and interpretive boundaries. Source identity and direct support are checked separately. Preview content is editorial draft; external sources are not represented as MARSAM publications. Relevant IAPOS distinctions between claims, citations, language and authorization inform the content design.

Proposed pre-publication process

Draft, check source identity, support, rights and currency, then review scientific content and translation separately. A responsible person approves a specific version. Material changes do not inherit prior approval automatically. No human editorial or translation-expert approval is claimed for this preview.

Accessible is not automatically reusable

Book chapters, scale items, images and recordings are not republished without checking usage rights. Publication records link to original sources. Private coursework, unpublished work and personal information are not included in this collection.

Privacy and accessibility

No accounts, tracking cookies or third-party analytics. Reading lists stay in the browser only when saved by the user. Search uses this site’s index. External sites have their own conditions. Keyboard navigation, visible focus, reduced motion and mobile layout are supported. This is not independent accessibility certification.

Corrections and version history

Identity, interpretation, translation or link issues prompt a new review. Git history distinguishes old and new versions. Material with unresolved rights or support issues is not promoted to approved publication. The contribution tool creates a local proposal only; it is not a private submission service.
